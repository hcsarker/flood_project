Why is Linear Regression's R² exactly 1.000?

Fitting an unregularized linear model on the raw (unscaled) features gives an intercept of 0 and a coefficient of exactly 0.005 for all 20 factors, with a maximum prediction error of ~4×10⁻¹⁶ (floating-point noise). In other words, this dataset's FloodProbability was generated as a perfectly equal-weighted average of the 20 severity scores — a useful finding to call out in your report. Random Forest scores lower (R²=0.646) precisely because tree splits approximate this smooth linear average less efficiently than a straight line does.
